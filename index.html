Research

FLARE. FunctionaL Annotation and REgularized Sparse Matrix Factorization for GWAS Summary Statistics

GWAS have generated extensive summary statistics across complex traits. Matrix factorization provides a natural way to summarize a high-dimensional variant-by-trait association matrix in terms of a smaller number of latent genetic factors. FLARE is designed to incorporate functional annotations directly into the factorization.

LASSO · Graph Laplacian · Factor Analysis

The Ukraine Youth and Family Longitudinal Study

This study follows 1,200 youth and 1,200 parents/caregivers, tracking their mental health, daily experiences, exposure to stress, and patterns of adaptation. As an analyst, I applied dyadic analysis to examine associations in war-related psychological distress within Ukrainian adolescent–parent dyads.

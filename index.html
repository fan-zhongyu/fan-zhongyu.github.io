FLARE. FunctionaL Annotation and REgularized Sparse Matrix Factorization for GWAS Summary Statistics

Why do very different traits sometimes share the same genetic signals? FLARE uses functional annotations and graph-regularized sparse factorization to uncover shared genetic structure in large collections of GWAS summary statistics. I developed the R/Rcpp algorithm, designed its simulations and benchmarks, and applied it across UK Biobank, FinnGen, All of Us, and Biobank Japan.

Modelling Floral Pattern Formation in Yeast Biofilms

This is part of my undergraduate dissertation. Why does a yeast colony sometimes grow petals instead of a boring circle? Here we put two published models side by side: reaction–diffusion to ask when a smooth edge becomes unstable and picks a petal spacing, and continuum mechanics to track how cells, extracellular matrix, and fluid set the colony’s radius and speed.

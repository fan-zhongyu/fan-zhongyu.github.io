Research

FLARE. FunctionaL Annotation and REgularized Sparse Matrix Factorization for GWAS Summary Statistics

GWAS have generated extensive summary statistics across complex traits. Matrix factorization provides a natural way to summarize a high-dimensional variant-by-trait association matrix in terms of a smaller number of latent genetic factors. FLARE is designed to incorporate functional annotations directly into the factorization.

LASSO · Graph Laplacian · Factor Analysis

The Ukraine Youth and Family Longitudinal Study

This study follows 1,200 youth and 1,200 parents/caregivers, tracking their mental health, daily experiences, exposure to stress, and patterns of adaptation. As an analyst, I applied dyadic analysis to examine associations in war-related psychological distress within Ukrainian adolescent–parent dyads.

ABM–SEIR Simulation Platform

What changes when people stop behaving like identical dots in an equation? This Shiny app lets individual agents meet, move through S–E–I–R states, and generate many possible epidemic curves. Run Monte Carlo scenarios, compare interventions, and watch small behavioural assumptions become population-level outcomes. The agents never cough; the confidence intervals still get busy.

Agent-based modelling · SEIR · Monte Carlo simulation

The Life of a Signal

How long does a signal stay alive once people begin to lose interest? Here a product launch, a public-health message, and an online rumor all enter the same three-state nonlinear network. You can play the model, bend its conditions, and watch attention spread, peak, and fade. No signal was harmed in the simulation; most were eventually ignored.

Nonlinear dynamics · Social networks

Site

Modelling Floral Pattern Formation in Yeast Biofilms

Why does a yeast colony sometimes grow petals instead of a boring circle? Here we put two published models side by side: reaction–diffusion to ask when a smooth edge becomes unstable and picks a petal spacing, and continuum mechanics to track how cells, extracellular matrix, and fluid set the colony’s radius and speed. No yeast was asked to solve PDEs; it still left us plenty of them.

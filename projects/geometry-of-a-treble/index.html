01

About

A season is usually compressed into a table. This work treats it as a changing spatial object. Each match redraws Barcelona from the origins of completed open-play passes; the triangle isolates Messi, Suárez and Neymar when all three appear.

The scope is deliberately exact: all 38 La Liga matches from 2014–15, followed by the Champions League final as an epilogue. It does not claim to represent the complete European campaign.

02

Method

A player’s position is the mean origin of his completed open-play passes, not a tracking-derived location. Lines count successful passes between visible players. The attack view retains possessions that reached the final third or ended in a shot, then draws one directional curve from the first action to the deepest point.

StatsBomb coordinates use a 120 × 80 field and orient the team in possession from left to right. Set pieces are excluded from the passing structure. Hover, tap or focus a player, shot path or match mark to reveal its values.

03

Extensions

The same grammar can follow individual goal sequences, compare score states, or add opponent pressure once equivalent event or tracking data is available. A future edition could also measure how the MSN triangle changes before and after substitutions rather than reducing each match to one centroid.
